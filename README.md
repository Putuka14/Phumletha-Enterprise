Phumletha Enterprise Website
Project Overview

The Phumletha Enterprise Website is a modern, responsive multi-page business website designed to provide customers with information about Phumletha Enterprise and allow them to enquire about available services.

The website focuses on three main areas:

Cash Loans
Goods Delivery Transport
"& Beyond" Business Services

The website is designed to work across desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones, providing customers with an easy and convenient way to access the business online.

Website Features
1. Home Page

The Home page provides an introduction to Phumletha Enterprise and highlights the main services offered.

It includes:

Company introduction
Welcome message
Main service categories
Call-to-action buttons
WhatsApp enquiry option
Responsive navigation
2. About Us

The About page provides information about Phumletha Enterprise, including:

Company background
Business vision
Business mission
Company values
Commitment to customers
3. Services

The Services page provides an overview of the different services offered by Phumletha Enterprise.

The main service categories include:

Cash Loans
Goods Delivery Transport
Business Services & Beyond
4. Cash Loans

The Cash Loans page provides customers with information about the company's loan service.

The page can include:

Loan service information
Basic requirements
Application information
Customer enquiry options
WhatsApp application/enquiry button

Note: Any final loan terms, fees, eligibility requirements and conditions should be confirmed by Phumletha Enterprise before being published on the website.

5. Goods Delivery Transport

The Goods Delivery Transport page describes the company's transportation and delivery services.

Services may include:

Goods delivery
Local transportation
Business deliveries
Customer-requested deliveries

Customers can contact the business through WhatsApp to enquire about delivery requirements and pricing.

6. "& Beyond" Business Services

The "& Beyond" section represents additional services provided by Phumletha Enterprise.

This section allows the company to showcase other business services and opportunities outside of cash loans and goods transportation.

Services can be expanded as the business grows.

7. Contact Us

The Contact page provides customers with different ways to communicate with Phumletha Enterprise.

It includes:

Contact form
WhatsApp enquiry
Business contact details
Email address
Business location, where applicable
Social media links, where applicable
WhatsApp Enquiry Form

The website includes a WhatsApp enquiry form that allows customers to enter their details and enquiry.

The form can collect information such as:

Customer name
Phone number
Service required
Enquiry/message

After submitting the form, the website can open WhatsApp with a pre-formatted enquiry message.

This provides customers with a fast and convenient communication channel.

Responsive Design

The website has been designed using responsive web development principles.

It supports:

Desktop computers
Laptops
Tablets
Android phones
iPhones
Other mobile devices

The navigation menu changes to a mobile-friendly menu on smaller screens.

Mobile Navigation

The website includes a responsive navigation system.

On desktop devices, users can access the main pages through the navigation bar.

On mobile devices, the navigation changes to a mobile menu to provide better usability on smaller screens.

Logo and Branding

The website uses the supplied Phumletha Enterprise logo as part of the company's branding.

The logo is displayed in appropriate areas such as:

Website header
Navigation
Home page
Footer
Business branding sections
